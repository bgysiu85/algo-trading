## Coding choices you should know about (Amendment 2, PRE-RUN, before any count)
- C1 one pending setup per market: a second same-side break while pending is ignored. Side effect: N=20 can produce FEWER entries than N=10.
- C2 opposite raw break beats a same-bar retest. C3 a close through the line fails the setup at once. C4 a retest on the last bar is not an entry.
- C5 S/R zone = pivots of the same kind that drew the line (highs for a resistance break), R=5, pivot bar in the 250 bars before t, cluster width 0.25 ATR[t], mean within 0.5 ATR[s] of the frozen line; filters the first retest entry only.
- Weekly filter is applied at the break bar t, not re-applied at the retest bar (the simulator's htf is set to the entry direction on retest bars).
- The registration's G4 says "pivots confirmed by t-1"; TL-v1's code and its cleared G5 use "confirmed by t". Coded the TL-v1 way; nothing after t enters.
