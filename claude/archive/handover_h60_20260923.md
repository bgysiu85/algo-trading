## Traps found while scoping — read before building

1. **The shared archive would be overwritten.** `common.databento_fetch` writes
   `<archive>/<dataset>/<schema>/<day>.dbn.zst` and REPLACES a day's file when the symbol list
   changes. `E:\Databento\XNAS.ITCH\ohlcv-1m\` is the small-cap pre-market archive. H60 bars live
   in `E:\Databento\H60\XNAS.ITCH\ohlcv-1m-rth\` and `data_plan.guard_archive` refuses the shared
   root. Do not point `databento_fetch` at H60 pairs.
2. **Databento `ohlcv-1h` is clock-aligned** (09:00–10:00 mixes pre-market with the open).
   TradingView's grid is 09:30-aligned. Hence 1-minute RTH resampled; fallback grid (§2.2,
   amendment B) only if the pull is > $50 or > 25 GB.
3. **EODHD codes are not Databento symbols:** `_OLD`/`_OLD1`/`_OLD2` stripped, `-` → `.`.
   Renamed and reused tickers are caught by the §5.1 cross-source check, not by mapping.
4. **EODHD `close` is split-adjusted, Databento is raw.** The ratio of the two is piecewise
   constant; steps > 20% are the split dates (holds across them are voided), and off-segment
   days > 1% are bad symbol-days. Stop rule at 2%.
5. **The `$2–20` band does not apply here** (large caps); the registration says why and puts a
   $5 floor and the cross-source check in its place.
6. **MCL's 3× volume clause on hourly bars** will favour the 10:30 bar (09:30 hour vs the
   previous 15:30 half-hour). Registered as an expectation to check, not to tune.
