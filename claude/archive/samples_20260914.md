## The feature space, read off the screenshots

The chart header is the most valuable thing in the zip. Indicators actually on
screen:

| on his chart | closest tested feature | same? |
|---|---|---|
| **EMA 9 close** | — | **never tested** |
| **EMA 200 close** | — | **never tested** |
| **BB 20 2 0 SMA** (bands) | `range_pct` (14-bar mean range) | different quantity |
| **Volume 20 SMA** | `vol_over_trail` (**60**-bar mean) | different lookback |
| VWAP hlc3 Session | `vwap_dist` | ✓ |
| MACD 12 26 close 9 | `macd_margin`, `macd_level` | ✓ |
| MFI 14 | `mfi`, `mfi_slope` | ✓ |
| — | `rsi`, `rsi_slope` | **RSI is not on his chart at all** |

Two structural findings follow immediately, with no run required:

1. **At least four quantities he watches have never been tested** — EMA9
   distance, EMA200 distance, Bollinger position/width, and volume against a
   **20**-bar mean rather than the 60-bar one the code uses. On a pre-market
   tape, 20 minutes and 60 minutes are not the same window.
2. **MCL requires RSI rising; Ben does not look at RSI.** The strategy carries
   a clause he does not use, and he uses measures the strategy does not have.

Timeframe is not fixed: CRBP is a **1-minute** chart, ARMP a **5-minute** one.
He switches, which means any rule fitted to one timeframe is fitted to half
his examples.

## Both `pass` examples share a shape, and it is not "nothing happening"

- **CRBP 07:19** — MFI **8.10**, volume 327 against a 20-SMA of 3.02K, price
  8.29 against session VWAP 9.25, MACD negative. The move is over and the tape
  has died.
- **ARMP 07:57** — MFI **100.00**, price ripping vertically to 6.75 with EMA9
  at 5.38 far below. The move is happening and he is too late.

Opposite ends of the same axis. A single threshold on MFI would take one of
these and refuse the other, which is a useful warning about fitting a rule to
one end of a distribution.

## The coverage problem, and the cheap fix

**Six of the eighteen are from 2026-09-14 — today — which is not in the
archive.** Confirmed: `E:\Databento\XNAS.BASIC\ohlcv-1m` has
`2026-06-16_0400_0930` but no `2026-09-14`.

| | |
|---|---|
| measurable now | **12** — 8 took, 3 took-and-lost, 1 blank |
| not measurable | **6** — SCNI, CRBP ×3, DRCT, ARMP |

**Both `pass` rows are in the unmeasurable six**, along with two of the three
"bought at peak" losses. The measurable set is therefore almost entirely
positives — which is the one composition that cannot support the comparison
this exercise exists for.

Pulling one day of `ohlcv-1m` for 2026-09-14 recovers all six. That is the
highest-value action available and it costs one request to price.

## What is NOT yet claimed

- Nothing has been measured. Everything above is read off chart headers and a
  spreadsheet.
- Eighteen samples against a 0.108% base rate cannot support a fitted rule.
  The design remains: place them in the distribution of all bars first, decide
  whether they cluster anywhere, and only then consider fitting — on half,
  tested on the other half, with `holdout.json` still shut.
- The four untested features were chosen **from his chart**, not from outcome
  data. That makes them a legitimate third experiment, and it has to be
  labelled as one: `entry_features` published 13 features, then 9 more, and
  these would be the next tranche with its own multiplicity count.
