## Summary

A small-account **scalping** methodology for forex and gold, discretionary and price-action/ICT-style (order
blocks, imbalances, break of structure) rather than a coded or backtested system.

### Account options presented
- **Personal account** — trade your own $40 directly; full freedom, smaller absolute profits, no evaluation gate.
- **Funded account** — put $40 toward a prop-firm-style evaluation to access a funded account (example figure
  cited: ~$5K buying power) if the evaluation is passed; higher profit potential but added evaluation constraints
  and rules.

### Timeframes
**15-minute** (context/setup) and **1-minute** (entry) — scalping rather than swing, with the explicit rationale
that a small account "cannot stand" the larger stop-losses swing trading requires.

### Three-step setup
1. Identify a high-probability range showing **weakness** (failed closes, wicks) followed by **strength** (a
   break of structure).
2. Wait for price to **pull back** into that range, toward a key level — an imbalance or order block.
3. Enter one of two ways:
   - **High reward-to-risk** entry — smaller risk per trade (0.25–1% of account).
   - **High win-rate** entry — larger risk per trade, capped at 3% max on funded accounts.

### Risk management principles stated
- Protect the capital base; avoid large drawdowns.
- Risk fixed percentages of account equity ("think in R and factors" — i.e. R-multiples).
- Only take asymmetrical (favorable reward:risk) setups.
- Evaluate the strategy's performance over a 50–100 trade sample before judging it.

### Examples shown
Live forex and gold trades with reward:risk ratios ranging **2.6:1 to 5:1** in the demonstrated setups.
