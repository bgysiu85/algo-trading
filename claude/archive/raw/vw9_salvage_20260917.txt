== 2. THE REQUIRED-PRECISION TEST ==
  non-top-3 pool: 1,515 trades   winners +36,540   losers (47,092)   net (10,552)
  333 winners / 1,182 losers  (22.0% win rate)

  An entry filter removes trades. Let p_w = fraction of WINNER dollars it removes,
  p_l = fraction of LOSER dollars. To make drop-top-3 non-negative:
        p_l*|L| - p_w*W  >=  |L| - W

  KEY RESULT: a filter uncorrelated with outcome has p_w = p_l = f, giving
        new_net = (W+L)*(1-f)
  which scales a negative number toward zero and NEVER crosses it.
  An uncorrelated filter cannot work at ANY strength. Selectivity s = p_l/p_w must exceed 1.

  Minimum removal required, by selectivity s:
     s       p_w (winner $ removed)   p_l (loser $ removed)
    1.10      69.1%                    76.1%
    1.25      47.3%                    59.1%
    1.50      30.9%                    46.4%
    2.00      18.3%                    36.6%
    3.00      10.1%                    30.2%
    5.00       5.3%                    26.5%
     inf      0.0%                     22.4%   (removes only losers)
