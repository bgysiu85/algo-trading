2. IT RE-FIRES EVEN WHEN PRICE TICKED DOWN SINCE THE LAST ALERT.
   DLXY    +0.00% between consecutive alerts
   DLXY    -5.43% between consecutive alerts
   DLXY    -2.46% between consecutive alerts
   DAIC    +0.00% between consecutive alerts
   DAIC    -1.97% between consecutive alerts
   ABTS    -2.70% between consecutive alerts

3. IT FIRES ON NAMES DEEPLY DOWN ON THE DAY.
   DLXY   gap  -49.78%   change-from-close as low as  -52.01%
   -> 'running up' is about the RECENT move, not the day's direction.
      Gap and change-from-close are display columns, not trigger inputs.
